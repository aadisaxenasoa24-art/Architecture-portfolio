# Antilia, Altamount Road, Mumbai: critical case study

A B.Arch case study of Antilia (Residence Antilia, 2006–2010) and its 1 km context on the Cumballa Hill ridge in South Mumbai.

## Contents

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `Antilia_research_dossier.md` | Five sourced research passes: building facts, land and legal record, critical perspectives, location and climate, neighbouring towers and precedents. Every claim carries its source and a confidence rating. |
| `antilia_1km_models.zip` | 1 km context model: Rhino 8 `.3dm` (layers, per-building user text), OBJ + MTL, glTF `.glb`, AutoCAD `.dxf` site plan, Google Earth `.kml`, building table `.csv` and a README on method and licences. |
| `antilia_imagery_2012-2025.zip` | Dated satellite frames (Esri World Imagery Wayback, 2012–2025) and Sentinel-2 annual mosaics, with capture dates, sensors and credits. |
| `antilia_drawings_renders_maps.zip` | Figure-ground, movement, open-space and height plates; a west–east section (SVG); model renders; Cook's Plan of Bombay (1887) and the 1909 Gazetteer map. |
